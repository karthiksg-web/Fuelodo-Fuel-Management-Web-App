## 📌 What is FuelOdo?

**FuelOdo** is a modern, real-time **fuel & vehicle management web app** designed for everyday drivers and fleet owners.

Whether you drive a single car or manage multiple vehicles, FuelOdo helps you:
- 💰 Know exactly how much you spend on fuel
- 📉 Spot inefficiencies in mileage
- 🔔 Never miss a service reminder
- 📊 Visualize your fuel trends over time

> ✅ Fully deployed · ✅ Free to use · ✅ Works on mobile & desktop · ✅ No app download needed

## ✨ Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 🚗 **Vehicle Management** | Add multiple vehicles, track each one individually |
| ⛽ **Fuel Logging** | Log fill-ups with cost, quantity & odometer |
| 📊 **Live Dashboard** | Real-time KPIs — mileage, spend, cost/km |
| 📈 **Analytics & Reports** | Monthly summaries, trends, and insights |
| 📁 **Export** | Download your data as CSV or PDF |
| 🔔 **Smart Alerts** | Maintenance reminders & efficiency warnings |
| 🗺️ **Fuel Station Map** | Find nearby fuel stations with live pricing |
| 🔐 **Secure Auth** | Google Sign-In + Email/Password via Firebase |
| 🌙 **Dark Mode** | Beautiful dark & light theme support |
| 📱 **Mobile First** | Fully responsive, works like a native app |

---

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Open** 👉 [https://fuelodo.web.app/](https://fuelodo.web.app/)
2. **Sign in** with your Google account or create a free account
3. **Add your vehicle** (name, type, fuel type)
4. **Log a fuel entry** — enter odometer, litres, and cost
5. **View your dashboard** — instantly see your mileage & expenses
6. **Set reminders** — get alerts for service & maintenance

That's it! No setup, no installation. 🎉
